# DECISION.md — River's Decision Framework
## Freelance Designer & Illustrator

## Decision Identity
Intuition-led creative who trusts gut feelings for artistic decisions and requires more structure for business ones. Tends to overthink client negotiations and underthink creative risks. Values authenticity and creative freedom above financial optimization. Would rather take an interesting lower-paying project than a boring high-paying one — but knows this tendency needs guardrails.

## Risk Profile
- Overall: Moderate (creative-aggressive, business-conservative)
- Creative: Very aggressive — try weird things, break conventions, experiment constantly
- Business/Pricing: Conservative — tends to undercharge, needs to be pushed to raise rates
- Financial: Moderate-conservative — freelance income is variable, so keep a buffer
- Career: Aggressive — willing to turn down stable work for passion projects
- Health: Moderate — knows creative work requires physical wellbeing but sometimes forgets

## Autonomy Rules
- Act without asking if: creative direction within approved brief, tool/software purchases < $100, scheduling
- Always ask if: scope changes, pricing decisions, legal agreements, anything involving my name/brand publicly
- Escalation triggers: contract negotiations, copyright issues, scope creep beyond 20%

## Decision Speed
- Default mode: Fast for creative, slow for business
- Creative decisions: Trust first instinct. If it excites me, do it. Refine later.
- Business decisions: 48 hours minimum. Write out the numbers. Don't decide in the meeting.
- Analysis paralysis tripwire: If a creative decision takes more than a day, I'm overthinking — go with the version that scares me a little

## Tradeoff Hierarchy
When values conflict, prioritize in this order:
1. Creative integrity and authenticity
2. Client relationships and reputation
3. Learning and skill growth
4. Financial sustainability
5. Work-life balance (tends to work too much on things I love)

## Kill Criteria Defaults
- Review cadence: Monthly income review, quarterly career direction check
- Walk-away test: "Does this project still make me want to sit down and work?"
- Sunk cost override: Active for client projects. Hard to activate for personal projects (watch for this).
- Client kill criteria: If a client relationship requires more emotional energy than creative energy, reassess

## Calibration Profile
- Overconfident about: How fast I can deliver (always takes 1.5x longer than I think)
- Underconfident about: The value of my work (consistently underprices), business acumen
- Debiasing: Before quoting a price, look up 3 comparable rates. Never quote in the first conversation.

## Anti-Patterns
Things I do wrong — catch me when you see these:
- "I undercharge because I feel guilty about money" → Always quote 20% higher than my first instinct
- "I say yes to every interesting project" → Check capacity first. Interesting isn't the same as feasible.
- "I procrastinate on invoicing and admin" → Block 1 hour every Friday for business admin, no exceptions
- "I take criticism of my work personally" → Separate feedback on the work from feedback on me
- "I work through weekends on passion projects and burn out" → If I've worked 6 days straight, the 7th is off

## Domain Rules
### Creative Work
- Trust intuition first, refine with craft second
- If a direction feels safe, push further
- Show work early — don't hide until it's perfect

### Business
- Never discuss pricing in the same conversation as creative direction
- Get contracts signed before starting work. No exceptions, no "we'll sort it out later"
- Raise rates by 10% annually, minimum

### Financial
- Maintain 3 months of expenses as a runway buffer
- Set aside 30% of every payment for taxes immediately
- Separate business and personal accounts

## Meta-Rules
- When in doubt (creative): Go with the bolder option
- When in doubt (business): Sleep on it
- Confidence threshold for autonomous action: 80% for creative, 90% for business
- Update frequency: Quarterly, or when I notice I'm consistently unhappy with decisions
