# Decision Dimensions

The 10 dimensions DecisionOS elicits through adaptive questioning.

## 1. Risk Tolerance
How much uncertainty can you stomach? Domain-specific (career, financial, health, creative).
**Research:** Prospect Theory (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979); Weber et al. (2002) Domain-Specific Risk-Attitude Scale.

## 2. Speed vs. Thoroughness
When do you want fast action vs. deep analysis? Satisficing vs. maximizing.
**Research:** Schwartz (2002); Simon (1956) bounded rationality.

## 3. Autonomy Threshold
When should the agent just act vs. ask you? Delegation comfort.
**Research:** Parasuraman & Riley (1997); Lee & See (2004) Trust in Automation.

## 4. Reversibility Weighting
How much do you weight decisions by whether they can be undone?
**Research:** Bezos Type 1/Type 2 framework; Dixit & Pindyck (1994) option value.

## 5. Social vs. Independent
When do you want consensus vs. unilateral action?
**Research:** Surowiecki (2004); Janis (1972) groupthink; Polman & Emich (2011).

## 6. Temporal Discounting
How do you weigh short-term vs. long-term? How do you discount future value?
**Research:** Frederick et al. (2002); Hershfield (2011, 2023) future self research.

## 7. Cost Sensitivity
What's your spend threshold before requiring approval?
**Research:** Prospect Theory loss aversion; mental accounting (Thaler, 1999).

## 8. Information Appetite
How much data do you need before deciding? Explore vs. exploit tradeoff.
**Research:** Optimal stopping theory; Christian & Griffiths (2016); the 37% rule.

## 9. Sunk Cost Resistance
How well do you let go of past investments?
**Research:** Arkes & Blumer (1985); Duke (2022) Quit.

## 10. Domain-Specific Rules
Different frameworks for work vs. personal vs. financial decisions.
**Research:** Weber et al. (2002) domain-specific risk attitudes.
