# Code of Conduct

DECISION.md is about making personal and organizational judgment explicit. That can involve sensitive topics: finances, health, work conflict, relationships, risk tolerance, and mistakes people are trying to stop repeating. Treat those contributions with care.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume contributors may be sharing anonymized versions of real experiences.
- Focus criticism on the format, examples, wording, evidence, or implementation.
- Flag privacy risks when an example appears to reveal sensitive information.
- Welcome different decision-making styles across cultures, domains, and life stages.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing private personal, financial, medical, legal, or workplace information.
- Dismissing a contributor's decision style as irrational without explaining the format or evidence issue.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban from participating in the project.

If you see a problem, open a private maintainer contact channel if one is listed in the repository. If no private channel exists, open a GitHub issue with the minimum information needed and avoid reposting sensitive details.
